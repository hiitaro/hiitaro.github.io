Common questions

How long does a website take?

It depends on the package and on how quickly we agree on content and feedback. You’ll get a clear timeline together with your quote.

How does payment work?

I’ll outline the price and the payment terms in writing before any work begins, so you always know what to expect.

Do I need a domain and hosting?

Every website needs both. They’re separate from the design fee. I can help you choose and set them up, or connect ones you already have.

Can I ask for changes to the design?

Yes. Your design goes through feedback rounds before launch, and the quote states how many are included.

Will you help after launch?

Yes. Small updates and ongoing maintenance are available as a separate service, so you’re never left to figure it out alone.

Are the sample projects real businesses?

They’re concept projects I designed and built to show my approach. The businesses are fictional. Yours would be designed around your own brand, services and customers.
