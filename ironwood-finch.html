Our Story

Old-world craft. Modern precision.

Ironwood & Finch began in 2011 as a two-chair room above a hardware store, with a single rule: nobody leaves rushed. Today our four barbers still work the traditional way — hot lather, steady hands, honest conversation — with all the precision a modern fade demands.

Every appointment ends the same: a warm towel, a clean neckline and a mirror check before you stand. It’s a small ritual, and it’s the reason our regulars never go anywhere else.
