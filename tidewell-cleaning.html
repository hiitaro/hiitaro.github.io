Questions, answered

Still unsure about something? Send a request below and we will reply within one business day.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

No. Many clients leave a key, a door code or a lockbox. We confirm access with you before the visit.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. Your cleaner brings supplies and equipment. If you prefer your own products, leave a note and we will use them.

Are your products safe for pets and kids?

We use plant-based, low-odor cleaners by default. Tell us about allergies or sensitivities and we will adjust.

What if something is not right?

Tell us within 24 hours and we will send a cleaner back to fix it at no cost.

How does pricing work?

Your price depends on the service, the size of your home and any extras. We confirm the final amount before your first visit, with no surprise fees.
